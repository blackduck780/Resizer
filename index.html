Before & After image size comparison

Always resize + convert your images before upload:

  • Original: 4.2 MB → 3840×2400 px
  • Good for web: ~180–450 KB → 1200–1600 px wide
  • Best for gallery: ~60–180 KB → 800–1200 px

Quick image optimization cheatsheet 2025/2026

# Using ImageMagick (best quality vs size)
convert input.jpg -quality 78 -strip -resize 1200x1200^ \
  -gravity center -extent 1200x1200 output.webp

# OR simpler – good enough for most cases
convert input.jpg -quality 82 -resize 1000x1000\> output.webp
